# EG-series publication boundary

This document fixes manuscript claim ownership across the eco-genetic research programme. It changes no scientific result, frozen endpoint, simulation, theorem, or evidence ledger.

## Programme-level question

What must be biologically distinguished, representationally preserved, predictively validated, and empirically measured before eco-genetic deterioration is forecastable?

The programme is organised as four inferential layers rather than as numbered sequel papers:

```text
mechanism and biological-state separation
    -> state representation for a declared forecast target and horizon
    -> predictive validation of a candidate warning rule
    -> empirical measurement / representation / identifiability gates
```

Each manuscript must stand alone and must not depend on publication of another manuscript for the validity of its own primary claim.

## EGC — mechanism and biological-state separation

Repository: `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-criticality`

Primary ownership:

- theorem-guided interaction-feedback and migration results;
- finite fragmentation effects under the declared closure;
- separation of potential high-trait viability, realised occupancy, interaction state, local effective size, diversity, allele persistence, and realised high-trait mass;
- the finding that distinct biological quantities need not share one fragmentation response shape;
- evidence-status discipline separating theorem, conditional result, and finite Type-S evidence.

Primary manuscript question:

> Which biological objects separate under fragmentation, and which conclusions are exact versus closure-conditional or finite?

Claim firewall:

- EGC owns **state separation**, not empirical or general state sufficiency.
- Avoid using `sufficient state` or `state sufficiency` as the parent manuscript's headline language.
- In `manuscript/main_text.md`, the sentence `Which state is sufficient therefore depends on the scientific target.` should be revised during the literature/manuscript pass to language such as `Which biological state is relevant to the scientific target therefore depends on the question being asked.`
- Historical H2-R remains event-conditioned ordering only and is not a predictive-warning headline.
- Full-denominator warning validity belongs to EGWE.

## EGWE state-validity lane — representation and forecast target/horizon

Repository: `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-warning-extensions`

Primary ownership:

- constructive insufficiency of coarse eco-genetic marginals for the declared next transition;
- cross-layer alignment as future-relevant structure under the declared closure;
- propagation of representation differences across fixed forecast horizons;
- distinction among next-transition relevance, paired trajectory identity, and marginal endpoint risk;
- process/operator-specific portability boundaries.

Primary manuscript question:

> Given biologically distinct states, what information must a representation retain to forecast a declared target at a declared horizon?

Claim firewall:

- This lane owns **state representation / sufficiency for declared targets and horizons**.
- It does not claim that cross-layer alignment explains the separate warning-validity failure.
- A non-significant long-horizon risk contrast is not equivalence or evidence of negligible effect.
- Connectivity parameters from distinct biological operators are not one transferable scalar state.

## EGWE warning-validity lane — signal and denominator

Repository: `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-warning-extensions`

Primary ownership:

- distinction between event-conditioned temporal precedence and full-denominator predictive discrimination;
- frozen full-denominator audit of the six relative-diversity rules;
- exact binary-marker denominator result connecting sensitivity, specificity, and AUC under perfect event-conditioned precedence.

Primary manuscript question:

> When does a precursor that occurs before failures actually qualify as a predictive warning?

Claim firewall:

- Warning failure is not attributed to missing cross-layer alignment without a separately preregistered bridge test.
- The result rejects the six frozen rules in the tested ensembles; it does not imply that genetic diversity contains no predictive information generally.

## EGWEE — empirical measurement and interpretation gates

Repository: `zuizui0223/egwee`

Primary ownership:

- endpoint-relevant measurement adequacy;
- information-preserving analytical representation;
- residual-context testing only after the supplied state earns predictive status;
- cross-study identifiability and fail-closed STOP outcomes.

Primary manuscript question:

> Before interpreting residual geography, habitat, origin, or history, has the proposed ecological state earned endpoint-relevant predictive status and has its analytical representation preserved the intended information?

Operational rule:

> **Test the state before interpreting the residual.**

Claim firewall:

- Heterogeneous natural systems are not pooled into one ecological effect.
- Natural-data outcomes do not validate the EGC/EGWE synthetic closure or the frozen genetic-warning rules.
- `not_identifiable`, `not_estimable`, failed proxy adequacy, representation collapse, and access STOPs remain scientific outcomes rather than missing results.

## Shared programme principle

The manuscripts are linked by one inferential principle:

> **Information for one inferential target does not guarantee adequacy for another.**

In particular:

- biological state variables are not interchangeable;
- matching marginal summaries need not imply matching next transitions;
- next-transition relevance need not imply an established signed long-horizon marginal-risk effect;
- paired trajectory-status sensitivity is not the same estimand as marginal incidence;
- event-conditioned temporal precedence is not predictive discrimination;
- a plausible empirical proxy is not automatically an endpoint-relevant state measurement;
- a richer raw measurement may cease to be richer after analytical representation.

## Publication presentation rule

The papers should not be titled or marketed externally as `Part I`, `Part II`, or `Part III`. Logical continuity may be explained in introductions, discussions, cover letters, preprints, and repository documentation, but each manuscript must expose its own question, evidence, and claim ceiling without borrowing validation from the other repositories.
