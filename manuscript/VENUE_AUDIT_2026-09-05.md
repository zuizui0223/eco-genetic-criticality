# EGC venue audit — 2026-09-05

## Decision

**Primary target: Theoretical Ecology.**

**Stretch alternative: The American Naturalist, Major Article.**

The decision is based on manuscript function rather than prestige alone. The active EGC paper combines exact results for declared maps, conditional ecological implications, and a finite stochastic closure to answer a broad ecological question: does fragmentation move one latent eco-genetic condition, or can interaction support, potential trait viability, realised occupancy, effective size and genetic summaries separate?

## Why Theoretical Ecology is the primary fit

The journal's stated editorial identity is theory used to answer ecological questions in a way that remains readable and relevant to a broad ecological audience. Its recent papers continue to include mathematical stability analysis, spatial ecological models and eco-evolutionary models. That is the native category of the present manuscript.

The EGC paper has three features that fit this scope particularly well:

1. **Exact mathematics is used to delimit, not inflate, ecological inference.** The canonical interaction map supplies a strict bistability condition; the network-mixing section supplies exact allele-frequency bounds.
2. **The finite model answers a biological state-separation question rather than merely exploring parameters.** The preregistered fragmentation gradient holds the prepared source state fixed within repeated comparisons and shows different response shapes for interaction, local effective size, realised high-trait mass, potential viability and realised occupancy.
3. **The theorem-to-closure distinction is itself part of the contribution.** Exact, conditional and finite numerical statements remain explicitly separated, making the paper suitable for a readership that evaluates how model assumptions map to biological conclusions.

## Why The American Naturalist is a stretch rather than the primary route

The American Naturalist explicitly seeks conceptual unification and papers that change how a broad organismal-biology readership thinks about a problem. Its Major Articles can contain new theory and are normally limited to 7,500 words and six combined figures/tables, with abstracts up to 200 words and double-anonymous review.

EGC could meet that bar if editors view **state separation under one structural perturbation** as a sufficiently broad conceptual contribution rather than as a model-specific theorem/closure study. The current manuscript has that possibility, but its strongest guaranteed fit remains theoretical ecology. Therefore AN is retained as a deliberate stretch route, not used to distort the manuscript into a more universal claim than the evidence supports.

## Submission framing for Theoretical Ecology

Working title:

> **Interaction thresholds and state separation under fragmentation: a theorem-guided finite-model framework**

One-sentence contribution:

> Fragmentation can separate interaction support, potential trait viability, realised occupancy, effective size and genetic summaries, so no single deterioration label can replace the biological state required by the question being asked.

The paper should be presented as an **Original/Research Article**, not as a warning paper, software paper, or omnibus EG-series paper.

## Main-display strategy

Use five main display items at most:

1. Figure 1 — theorem/closure/state-separation architecture;
2. Figure 2 — exact analytical boundaries: interaction bistability and migration bounds;
3. Figure 3 — preregistered fixed-source fragmentation-gradient design and realised response curves;
4. Figure 4 — potential viability versus realised occupancy state separation;
5. Table 1 — evidence labels and biological state non-equivalences.

The detailed patch-count ledger, warning-history bookkeeping and additional theorem diagnostics belong in the supplement. Historical H2-R/H2-A warning material remains non-headline and must not be used to enlarge the paper.

## Claim ceiling

The submission does not claim:

- a universal fragmentation dose-response;
- that the canonical sigmoid threshold is a theorem for arbitrary ecological systems;
- that allele-frequency mixing is demographic, pollen, seed or pollinator movement;
- that potential viability and realised occupancy share a universal lag;
- predictive warning validity;
- forecast-state sufficiency, which belongs to the EGWE state-validity paper;
- natural-system validation of the finite closure.

## Remaining non-scientific blockers

Before external submission, author-controlled metadata still have to be supplied: author list/order, affiliations, corresponding author, funding, competing interests, final AI/tool disclosure and permanent archive/repository identifiers. These are submission metadata, not reasons to reopen the model campaign.
